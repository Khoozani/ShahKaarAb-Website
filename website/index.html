Shah Kaar Ab

Shah Kaar Ab is the official website of Shahaboddin Habibi Khoozani, an Independent Game Auteur and creator of independent game software, interactive works, digital experiences, and cultural projects.

The website presents his games, projects, research, articles, cultural collaborations, lectures, exhibitions, and selected works.

Based in Kashan, Iran, Shah Kaar Ab brings together art, technology, game design, software, history, culture, and interactive media.
