Shah Kaar Ab — Independent Game Auteur

Shah Kaar Ab (شاه کار آب) is the personal creative identity of Shah-ab Khoozani, an Independent Game Auteur who intentionally creates games centered on civilization, culture, and heritage.

His work explores civilization and cultural heritage through game design, interactive media, digital works, and software, bringing together art, technology, history, and interactive experiences.

Games Centered on Civilization, Culture & Heritage

Shah Kaar Ab is the official website of Shah-ab Khoozani, an Independent Game Auteur whose work is intentionally centered on civilization, culture, and heritage.

Rather than treating cultural and historical subjects simply as visual inspiration, Shah Kaar Ab approaches civilization and cultural heritage as a deliberate creative focus for game design and interactive works.

His projects explore history, cultural heritage, traditional knowledge, and civilizational themes through games, interactive experiences, software, digital works, research, and cultural collaborations.

Based in Tehran and Kashan, Iran, Shah Kaar Ab brings together art, technology, game design, software, history, culture, and interactive media.
